## Liquid culture (4% sugar)

- 1000ml (1 cup) water
- 20 ml ( or 12 tsp) Glucose
- 2 gram (or 2 tsp) Extra Light Malt Extract
- pH 6.5

## Agar: Malt extract

### Ingredients

- Malt extract 20 g
- Agar agar 20 g
- Glucose 2g
- Distilled water 1000 ml

Method:

- Dissolve malt extract in a plastic beaker and pH the solution to pH 6.5 with NaOH.
- Soak agar in small quantity of solution. Bring remaining solution to the boil, stirring constantly.
- Add to soaking agar. Bring to boil, stirring constantly.

Autoclave at 121C for 10-15 minutes, remove and slope or pour for plates as required.
